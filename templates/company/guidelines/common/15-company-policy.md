### Company policy

- Text that ships outside the company (README, published docs, package metadata, help output,
  public changelog, error messages shown to customers) must not contain internal ticket
  identifiers, internal hostnames or private repository names. **Major** when the reference
  resolves only from inside the company network; **Minor** when it is merely a dead reference.
  Verify by resolving the name or link from outside.
- A change description that cites no ticket or issue is not a finding; the review owner tracks
  that elsewhere.
